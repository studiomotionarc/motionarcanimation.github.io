01 / THE PROJECT

WELCOME TO STΛRLIGHT

A small seaside town.
A much bigger mystery.

Starlight is an original animated story inspired by the warmth, emotional storytelling and fantasy of shows like Steven Universe, reimagined for an older audience. Designed for viewers aged 16 and up, it blends humor, warmth and wonder with identity, grief, family, loss, love, friendship, trauma and the fear of not knowing who you really are.

At its center is Noah Vale, a sixteen-year-old who has grown up under the protection of three ancient magical beings. When powers inherited from his missing mother begin to awaken, the ordinary life he wants starts to collide with a supernatural history that someone has worked very hard to keep hidden.

03 / THE SOUND

THE MUSIC OF STΛRLIGHT

When words fail,
let the story sing.

Music is a huge part of Starlight. Original songs will reveal what characters cannot always say aloud — from love and loss to fear, secrets and the strange experience of becoming someone you don't yet understand.

The score and songs will change with the story. Some moments will be fun and upbeat. Others will be romantic, dark, emotional or uncomfortable. Musical themes can represent characters and events, becoming part of the world rather than something separate from it.
